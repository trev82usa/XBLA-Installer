**XBLA Installer** gets the game files out of your own Xbox Live Arcade package, ready for a PC port. No Python, no command line.

- **Windows:** download `XBLA-Installer.exe` and double-click it.
- **Linux:** download `XBLA-Installer-linux-x64.tar.gz`, extract, run `./XBLA-Installer`.

Pick your game package, pick the port's folder, press **Install**. It recognises the game, extracts and verifies everything, and for known ports (Trials HD, Guardian Heroes, Root Beer Tapper, The Simpsons Arcade) checks that your `default.xex` is the version the port needs and can apply a title update. Works on any Xbox 360 STFS package (LIVE / CON / PIRS) as a plain extractor too.
