**XBLA Installer** gets the game files out of your own Xbox 360 games, ready for a PC port. No Python, no command line.

- **Windows:** download `XBLA-Installer.exe` and double-click it.
- **Linux:** download `XBLA-Installer-linux-x64.tar.gz`, extract, run `./XBLA-Installer`.

**New in 1.1.0**
- **Shows which version you have** as soon as you pick a file: the game, its title ID and version, and for known ports whether it's the right version or needs the title update. Works on packages, disc images, or a `default.xex` on its own.
- **Just extract:** tick it to pull everything out of any package or ISO into any folder, with no game checks. Leave the folder empty and it goes next to the file, named after the game.
- **Disc images (.iso):** original Xbox and Xbox 360 ISOs (XGD1, XGD2, XGD3) are extracted too, not just Xbox Live Arcade packages.

Pick your game package or ISO, pick the port's folder, press **Install**. For known ports (Trials HD, Guardian Heroes, Root Beer Tapper, The Simpsons Arcade) it checks `default.xex` is the version the port needs and can apply a title update.
